Pictures used by the Sakura theme (src/index.css, rules under :root[data-theme='sakura']).
Every source is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), so no attribution is required; it is
given here anyway. Each picture was cut out of its photo's sky and resized for this theme; the
colours are the photos' own, with the blossoms lifted slightly.

branch-right.avif, branch-right.webp, branch-left.avif, branch-left.webp,
branch-top.avif, branch-top.webp
  Flowering cherry branches against a clear sky, framing Mount Fuji. The sky was keyed out
  and the top-right and top-left branches cropped; branch-top is the left one, flipped and
  cut shorter for phones.

  Author:  JJ Ying (https://unsplash.com/@jjying)
  Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (Commons' {{Unsplash}} tag, which applies {{Cc-zero}}; posted to
           Unsplash in March 2017, when its photos were published under CC0)

fuji.avif, fuji.webp
  Mount Fuji above Lake Kawaguchi on a clear winter day. The sky was removed and the foot of
  the mountain faded into haze; the mountain keeps its natural colour.
  Source:  "Fuji Kawaguchi 357.JPG"
           https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fuji_Kawaguchi_357.JPG
  Author:  Marion & Christoph Aistleitner

petal-1.avif, petal-1.webp, petal-2.avif, petal-2.webp
